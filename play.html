About Dropwell

Dropwell is an original falling-block puzzle from Northwell Studio. Glyphs fall into a 10-wide well. You rotate, shift, and drop them. Full lines disappear. The well gets faster. If the stack crosses the rim, the round is over.

It sits in a familiar genre. The name, look, and this site are ours.

Objective

Score by clearing horizontal lines. A line vanishes when every cell is filled. Levels rise as lines go. Glyphs fall faster. Stack over the top and it’s game over.

Controls & scores

Single = 100 × level. Double = 300 × level. Triple = 500 × level. Well (four lines) = 800 × level. Soft drop is 1 point per cell. Hard drop is 2. The ghost shows the landing. Next shows what’s coming. Hold parks one glyph until you need it.

How it works

Glyphs come from a seven-bag shuffle, so each shape shows once per bag. Wall kicks let you rotate in tight stacks. A short lock delay lets you slide after a landing. Hold once per drop. The well is 10 cells wide and 20 cells tall.
